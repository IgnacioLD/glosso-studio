Stop being asked to repeat yourself. Whether you're learning English, improving your accent for work, or adapting to life in a new country, Glosso gives you the feedback you need to speak with confidence.

★ INSTANT PRONUNCIATION FEEDBACK
Speak into the app and get visual, word-by-word feedback on your accuracy. See exactly which sounds you mispronounce and fix your accent in real time.

★ LEARN THE SOUNDS (IPA)
Toggle phonetic transcription (IPA) to see the exact pronunciation of every word. Understand the sounds instead of mimicking them - the fastest route to a natural accent.

★ 100% PRIVATE & OFFLINE
Practice anywhere: on a plane, on the subway, at home. Glosso processes your voice entirely on-device. No internet, no account, no tracking, no ads. Your audio never leaves your phone.

★ STRUCTURED COURSES - 6 LEVELS
Follow a clear path from beginner to advanced, from everyday phrases to complex professional speech. Hundreds of sentences across topics that matter.

★ REAL-WORLD TOPICS
Daily life, job interviews, business, law, economics, travel, shopping and more - practice the language you'll actually use.

★ NATIVE REFERENCE VOICES
Listen to high-quality native voices to pick up natural rhythm, intonation and word stress.

★ SPACED REPETITION
Difficult sentences come back automatically until you master them. Turn pronunciation mistakes into strengths.

★ MASTERY SCORE & STREAKS
Track your progress and keep your daily practice streak alive. Consistency is the secret to a clear accent in any language.

★ MULTIPLE LANGUAGES
Practice English (UK and US), French, Spanish, German and Latin. Switch between languages anytime - each has its own curriculum, native reference voices and phonetic feedback. Latin is included as an experimental bonus.
